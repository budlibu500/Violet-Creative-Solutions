# Violet Creative Solutions

I organised the features around how a client would use the site, from choosing a service to sending a commission request. I gave high priority to the steps needed to find work in the portfolio and contact the studio. Comparing sketches and adding a deadline can come later.

| ID | Feature | What the visitor can do | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | About the studio | Read about the team and the types of work we take on. | High |
| F02 | Choose a service | Compare Living Ink, Flow State, Still Life and Abstract Landscape and choose a service. | High |
| F03 | Browse the portfolio | Look through images of completed projects. | High |
| F04 | Filter by service | Select a service to narrow the gallery. Selecting Living Ink shows the character art. | High |
| F05 | View a project | Open a larger image and read what the client originally asked for. | High |
| F06 | Compare sketch and final piece | Switch between the first sketch and the finished piece to see what changed. | Medium |
| F07 | Use a reference | Select a piece from the portfolio to include in the commission form. | Medium |
| F08 | Describe a commission | Choose a service and describe the work you would like the studio to make. | High |
| F09 | Add a deadline | Enter a date if you need the finished piece for a particular occasion. | Medium |
| F10 | Send the request | Add a reply email and submit the form. The form confirms the request or shows which required field still needs an entry. | High |
